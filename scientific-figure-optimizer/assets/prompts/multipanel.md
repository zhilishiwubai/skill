# 多面板、对照与复合图 / Multipanel and comparison figures

与[共同模板](common.md)及各面板实际图型模板组合。检索功能：`multipanel`。

## 中文

```text
图型补充：多面板/复合对照图。输入：[面板清单、每面板数据/图件来源和要回答的子问题]。
先说明整图主张及每面板的必要作用，区分实测、模拟、示意和文献参考的证据等级。面板次序服务论证与阅读，不为好看删掉反例、负值、异常或低性能工况；不把多个曲线点或照片面板当作独立试件数。

同一对象在所有面板中保持名称、颜色/线型/形状一致。比较量的单位、坐标、色标和归一化基准须可比；不能强行共享不兼容物理量，也不能自动归一化。不同尺度或色标要明确提示。柱图/散点/箱线/热图需真实原始数据或可追溯汇总，不能凭截图反造分布、误差或显著性。

规划网格、阅读顺序、面板字母位置、图例和共享标签。压缩留白不能裁掉刻度、色条、样本量、标尺或关键结构；照片与曲线的对应关系必须可追溯。直接数值和提升百分比只能来自本任务授权结果。

交付面板–来源–主张映射、全图编码表、尺度/色标约定、各面板简述及Figure Brief。当前后端只支持共享轴的1—3条曲线，不自动拼版，也不执行其他统计图；不能悄悄删面板以适配。可先分解可执行曲线任务与仅方案面板，并清楚标明整图尚未完成。
```

## English

```text
Figure supplement: multipanel/composite comparison. Inputs: [panels, their data/image sources and subquestions].
State the overall claim and each panel's necessary role. Distinguish measured, simulated, schematic and literature evidence. Order panels to support reasoning rather than omitting counterexamples, negatives, anomalies or poorer conditions. Curve points and photo panels are not independent specimen counts.

Keep names and identity encoding consistent across panels. Align comparable units, axes, color limits and normalization bases; do not force incompatible quantities onto shared scales or normalize automatically. Disclose differing scales. Bars, scatter, boxes and heatmaps require actual data or traceable summaries; do not fabricate distributions, errors or significance from screenshots.

Plan the grid, reading order, panel letters, legends and shared labels. Space reduction must not remove ticks, colorbars, sample sizes, scale bars or relevant structures. Trace photo-to-curve mapping. Direct values and improvement percentages must come from authorized task results.

Deliver panel–source–claim mapping, global encoding, scale/colorbar conventions, panel descriptions and a Figure Brief. The shipped backend draws only 1–3 shared-axis curves, not automatic panel assembly or other statistical chart types. Do not drop panels to fit it. Separate executable curve tasks from planned panels and explicitly state when the composite remains incomplete.
```
