# 研究照片证据整理 / Research-photo evidence review

与[共同模板](common.md)组合。此项是证据审阅方式，不是新增检索类别或照片编辑后端。

## 中文

```text
图型补充：研究照片/试件图证据整理。输入：[原始照片、来源、试件/工况/阶段与数据映射记录、使用权及允许调整范围]。
先区分研究证据照片与仅供表达参考的图片。核对原件、哈希、拍摄对象、时间/方向信息和数据对应；对应不完整时保持UNMAPPED，不根据相似外观补试件编号、破坏阶段或实验结论，也不将它混入已审论文案例计数。

只描述实际可见的构件、尺度线索、损伤和遮挡，区分观察、解释与未知。标尺必须由真实标定支持；不能靠器物常识生成精确长度。标注指向可见位置，不凭照片声称内部裂纹、材料性质或不可见结构。

如需用于装置/结构/多面板表达，检索对应科学功能的参考，而非传入不存在的photos检索参数。规划标注、方向、排列和图注；记录每个面板的来源、映射及版权状态。若讨论裁切、旋转或亮度方案，说明将保留的证据与变化依据，保留原件；没有当前有效工具授权时不执行图像编辑。

不得移除裂纹、缺陷、接触、遮挡或不利结果，不补画损伤、结构或标尺。当前只交付证据清单、映射缺口、可见性观察、标注/排版方案和作者待确认项；不把清晰化、生成图或美化当作证据升级。
```

## English

```text
Figure supplement: research-photo/specimen-image evidence review. Inputs: [original photos, provenance, specimen/condition/stage/data mapping, rights and allowed changes].
Separate research evidence from visual references. Check originals, hashes, subjects, time/orientation information and data links. Incomplete mapping remains UNMAPPED; visual similarity cannot establish specimen IDs, failure stages or conclusions. Do not count such photos as reviewed paper figures.

Describe only visible components, scale cues, damage and occlusions, distinguishing observation, interpretation and unknowns. Scale bars need actual calibration, not familiar-object guesses. Annotations must point to visible features; photographs do not prove hidden cracks, material properties or internal structure.

For apparatus, structure or composite presentation, retrieve the relevant scientific function instead of supplying an unsupported photos search parameter. Plan labels, orientation, arrangement and captions, recording each panel's provenance, mapping and rights. Discuss crop/rotation/brightness changes only with evidence-preservation rationale and originals retained; without valid current tool authorization, do not execute edits.

Do not remove damage, defects, contacts, occlusions or unfavorable evidence, and do not invent damage, structures or scale bars. This route delivers an evidence register, mapping gaps, observations, annotation/layout plan and author questions. Sharpening, generation or beautification does not raise evidence grade.
```
