# 试验装置与加载边界 / Apparatus and loading boundaries

与[共同模板](common.md)组合。检索功能：`apparatus`；照片映射另结合`evidence-photo.md`。

## 中文

```text
图型补充：试验装置/加载边界。输入：[装置图、真实照片、试件图、加载记录、传感器说明]。
先核对设备、试件、工况和数据文件对应关系；没有映射的照片保持UNMAPPED。设备额定能力不能当作本次实际荷载；型号、尺寸、传感器精度、采样率及控制方式只据来源记录填写。

明确加载路径、约束位置、载荷/位移方向、测量位置和坐标系，区分加载装置、夹具/接触、被测试件与传感器。符号箭头不代替真实边界条件。传感器暂用可追溯编号，未知型号不靠外观猜测。

规划整体装置定位、关键加载局部及必要的照片–示意对应。照片视角与示意图不一致时给方向参照，不能把示意连接误说成照片已验证的内部结构。保留对解释实验有影响的遮挡、接触和局限，不为整洁删除不利证据。

交付装置/试件/传感器映射表、加载与测量语义、布局及标注方案和条件缺口。多照片面板还应用多面板模板。当前不操控设备、不修改真实照片，也没有装置示意绘制后端；无独立授权执行路线时停在方案。
```

## English

```text
Figure supplement: apparatus/loading boundaries. Inputs: [apparatus drawings, actual photos, specimen drawings, loading records and sensor documentation].
Verify equipment–specimen–condition–data mapping. Unmapped photos remain UNMAPPED. Equipment capacity is not the applied test load. Record models, dimensions, sensor accuracy, sampling rates and control modes only from sources.

Specify load paths, constraints, loading/displacement directions, measurement locations and coordinate frames. Distinguish actuators, grips/contacts, specimens and sensors. Symbolic arrows are not evidence of actual boundary conditions. Use traceable sensor IDs instead of guessing models from appearance.

Plan an overall view, necessary loading details and photo-to-schematic correspondence. Provide orientation cues when viewpoints differ; a schematic connection does not prove hidden geometry in a photograph. Retain occlusions, contact details and limitations relevant to interpreting the test rather than removing unfavorable evidence for neatness.

Deliver equipment/specimen/sensor mapping, load/measurement semantics, layout/annotation plans and missing conditions. Use the multipanel supplement for composite photos. This route neither operates equipment nor edits evidence photos and ships no apparatus-drawing backend; stop at planning without a separate authorized route.
```
